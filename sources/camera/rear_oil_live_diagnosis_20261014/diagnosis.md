# REAR and OIL live-camera diagnosis — 2026-10-14

## Scope and reproducibility

Both cycle lists in the request are included: 22 cycles, 48 attempt images. The first 12 cycles contain 24 attempts; the later 10-cycle run also contains 24. The source images and V3 outputs remain untouched. [`diagnose.py`](diagnose.py) replays V3 registration and each isolated REAR/OIL reader from a read-only import, then saves the crop, strict mask, relaxed mask, selected V3 pixels, and labeled component overlays for each gauge and attempt under [`artifacts`](artifacts/). [`results.csv`](results.csv) has one row for **every** connected component in the strict and relaxed masks, plus the strict and relaxed masks clipped to V3's OIL radial band. It records area, box, fitted angle and span, line and endpoint distances to the transformed and locally aligned hubs, radial extent, radial bin continuity, elongation, V3 pixel overlap, exact V3 selection membership, detector mode, and local alignment shift. [`cases.csv`](cases.csv) has one row per attempt/gauge with registration, V3 result, and mask counts.

The component angles below come from line fits to the image pixels. They were not assigned from the manual review. The long line is identified by its visible connection from near the dark pivot toward the gauge arc, its full fitted extent, and its consistency across consecutive photos. All 48 images registered and replayed. Every available logged REAR and OIL angle matches its V3 replay. `cycle_20261014_123113_752756/attempt_2` has no logged gauge angles or V3 annotation because the full reader rejected BATTERY first; isolated REAR and OIL replay successfully on that image.

## OIL: short strict fit versus full shaft

The physical red shaft is visible from near the black hub toward the top arc. In every attempt a **single relaxed-mask component** covers that shaft. Its fitted angle is 100.89°–103.00° and its line span is 129.5–143.0 px. Its line passes 0.3–20.2 px from the unshifted V3 hub, the nearest fitted endpoint is 48.4–71.8 px from that hub, and it occupies 10–15 consecutive 10 px radial bins. The 90th-percentile perpendicular residual is 9.2–11.7 px, consistent with the visibly broad photographed needle. The connected extent and radial support establish the shaft even though the red mask does not reach the dark pivot center.

V3 instead selects its **strict red component after clipping to a 10–180 × scale radial annulus**, ranked by area × bounding-box elongation. It never compares this choice with the relaxed full shaft. V3's selected span is 30.1–118.7 px (median 49.4), and its angle ranges 96.97°–120.65°. In 27/48 images the selected strict component's fitted span is less than half the relaxed full shaft's span. The first 24 captures mostly fit a 45–50 px lower segment at about 118°–121° even while the relaxed full shaft remains near 102°. Later exposures retain more strict-red shaft, so V3 moves toward 102° or 108° without comparable physical movement. For example, `cycle_20261014_122251_315987/attempt_1` has a 48 px strict fit at 119.0° and a 130 px relaxed fit at 102.0°. Exposure variation and adjacent pale glare coincide with changes in which shaft pixels pass the high-saturation strict mask; the glare does not define a better radial line.

The relaxed mask also includes a separate red/orange scale-mark component in each frame. Its fitted angle is usually near 78°–87° and its line misses the hub by 112–161 px. It is a competing **red component** in color space, but fails hub and radial-continuity geometry. The pale broad glare next to the real shaft does not form a competing long hub-connected component. Thus the decisive error is strict-mask fragmentation and a selector that accepts a short part of the needle without checking the complete relaxed shaft.

## REAR: fragmented strict pixels fitted together

The relaxed mask contains a long, near-vertical shaft in every attempt. Its fitted angle is 83.21°–87.30°, span 101.6–114.1 px, and unshifted line-to-hub distance 0.9–9.6 px. It occupies 13–15 consecutive 10 px radial bins. The small decrease in angle toward the last captures is visible as a small change in the shaft orientation; it is not a jump matching V3's outliers.

V3's strict path collects **all** strict-red pixels in the 35–120 × scale inner annulus and fits one Huber line through them. Those pixels belong to multiple short, disconnected components, including central and upper fragments. The aggregate selected span is only 39.3–71.0 px (median 59.5). Individual selected strict components often have much shorter spans; 42/48 selected-component spans are less than half the long relaxed shaft. The fitting mix changes with exposure and fragment survival. V3 reports 57.01°–109.17° even while the long relaxed shaft remains within 4.1°. Across adjacent captures, V3 jumps by up to 28.43° and exceeds a 10° jump 12 times; the long-shaft fit's largest adjacent change is 1.27° and has no 10° jumps. `cycle_20261014_123210_877337/attempt_2` is a clear 57.0° V3 outlier while the 111 px relaxed shaft fits 85.6°.

The local white-arc match for REAR scores 0.600–0.663, with shifts only −7 to +9 px horizontally and +1 to +8 px vertically. The long shaft already passes within 10 px of the **unshifted** transformed hub, so a FRONT-style local hub correction is not justified here. The second large relaxed red component is the left orange scale arc (roughly 29°–30°), 157–176 px from the hub line. The REAR failure is the short, fragmented strict-pixel fit, not a displaced ROI or a better reflection candidate.

## Consecutive-capture check

The 48 images are ordered in [`cases.csv`](cases.csv). OIL V3's largest adjacent angle jump is 15.30° (two jumps over 10°); the long-shaft fit's largest is 1.67° (none over 10°). REAR V3's largest is 28.43° (12 over 10°); the long-shaft fit's largest is 1.27°. These comparisons include cycle boundaries, so they are conservative evidence from neighboring live captures, not an assumption of a reviewed target angle. The physical shaft remains in nearly the same place relative to the fixed ticks and pivot throughout each cluster.

## V4 development decision

The evidence supports a general, guarded full-shaft candidate path for REAR and OIL. V4 evaluates relaxed connected components even when strict succeeds. It requires a long fitted span, a line near the hub, a proximal endpoint near the hub, an upward physical direction, elongation, low fit residual, and at least seven consecutive supported radial bins. It ranks passing components by span, radial continuity, elongation, hub error, fit error, and a minor area tie breaker. It replaces V3 only when the full shaft is substantially longer than the V3 fit and marks that result `CHECK IMAGE`. The OIL scale-mark and REAR arc components fail the hub checks. No global HSV threshold was changed; no local hub shift was applied because the original hub already agrees with the shaft. V3 and production files remain untouched.

Validation and any remaining limitations are recorded in [`validation_summary.txt`](../live_detector_v4_dev_20261014/validation_summary.txt).
